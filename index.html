RESEARCH PROTOCOL / AA—001

How to read this study

This app is about avoiding false confidence, not selling a profitable signal.

1. Market data

Daily 23:00 UTC close sampled from a third-party mirror of Binance BTC/USDT USD-M futures hourly OHLCV, 2024–2025. The original upstream file is not independently exchange-verified. No live prices and no order book.

2. Selection protocol

Ten rules are defined before testing: momentum or reversal based on previous 2, 5, 10, 20 or 40 daily observations. The highest Sharpe in 2024 is locked as the champion. Jan–Jun 2025 is a validation diagnostic. Jul–Dec 2025 is the final holdout.

3. Timing

Position for the (t−1,t] return is computed using prices no later than the end of t−1. Turnover costs are debited when the new position is entered. An outright flip costs two units of turnover.

4. Statistical caution

Annualised Sharpe uses sqrt(365) because the reference data has daily crypto bars. The 95% moving-block bootstrap interval refers to the mean daily return, not the compounded terminal return. The circular-shift placebo has limitations and is not a formal p-value for alpha.

5. What we do not claim

No leverage constraints, borrow/funding charges, live spreads, transaction-level fees, execution slippage estimates, market impact or forward performance are validated here. Any interactive retuning after seeing the holdout destroys its status as an untouched test for the new choice.
